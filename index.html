About

A PhD-trained AI engineer, I have spent over ten years in research and industry. Most of my recent work has been in ecology, at the Alpine Ecology Lab (CNRS). There I built language-model pipelines to extract information from large document collections, knowledge graphs to connect data across sources, and open-source tools. Other projects included data pipelines for large citizen-science datasets and models that identify animals from sound. Earlier, my work covered real-time AI for audiovisual production.

Away from the keyboard: in 2025 I ran a small tea and specialty coffee shop in Pau, which taught me a lot about customers and cash flow. I also teach algorithmics and complex problem solving to engineering students at EPSI.
