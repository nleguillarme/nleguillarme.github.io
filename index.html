01 Statement

My work turns complex, messy information into knowledge teams can act on: language models, knowledge graphs, and the engineering to make them hold up in real conditions.

Listening first, the simplest method that works, and tools shaped by their users: that's how I make AI useful to those who need it.

What the work is for counts for me, so I aim it at problems that matter to people and the planet.
